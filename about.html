Our Story

More Than Traffic Control.
It's About People.

Ryan Robicheau’s journey as a business owner began long before Blacktop Traffic Service, rooted in the hard work of his grandfather’s mink ranch and a lifelong entrepreneurial spirit. After gaining years of hands-on experience in traffic control and management, Ryan officially launched Blacktop in 2022. Built on a foundation of family support and local collaboration with organizations like CBDC Digby-Clare and Nova Scotia Works, the company has rapidly grown from a solo vision into a dedicated team of professionals serving all of Nova Scotia.

Beyond the road, Ryan’s commitment to safety and community is personal. As a Captain and Medical First Responder with the Plympton and Gilbert’s Cove Fire Department, he brings a high-stakes level of discipline and care to everything he does. Blacktop is more than just a traffic service; it is a family-supported business driven by a mission to provide quality jobs and essential safety services. Every project is guided by the same hands-on work ethic and community-first mindset that have defined Ryan’s career from the very beginning.

Our Mission

Safety Is Not a Service.
It's a Standard.

Our mission is to set the standard for traffic control in Nova Scotia through rigorous training, unwavering professionalism, and a deep respect for every life that passes through our work zones. We exist to create an environment where road workers can focus on their jobs and the travelling public can move through safely and confidently.

We measure our success not by the number of projects we complete, but by the number of people who make it through safely. That's the standard we hold ourselves to, every crew, every site, every day.
