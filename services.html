What We Do

Our Services

Comprehensive traffic management solutions designed to keep Nova Scotia's roads safe and projects on schedule. Every service is delivered by trained, certified professionals.

Traffic Control

Full-spectrum traffic control planning and execution for construction zones, utility work, and road maintenance projects throughout Nova Scotia. Our certified traffic control persons ensure compliance with provincial regulations and MUTCDC standards.

Training

Comprehensive traffic control safety training for workers and supervisors. Our certified instructors deliver hands-on courses covering flagging procedures, work zone safety, MUTCDC standards, and provincial regulations to ensure your team is prepared and compliant.

Traffic Control

Keeping Roads Safe, On Every Job

From highway construction to utility work, our certified team delivers professional traffic control solutions tailored to each project. Here's what we bring to the table.

Flagging Operations

Certified flaggers trained to safely direct traffic through active work zones, keeping workers and the public protected at all times.

Lane Closures

Strategic lane closure setup using signage, barriers, and delineators to redirect traffic while maintaining safe flow through work areas.

Traffic Management Plans

Custom traffic management plans developed to meet provincial requirements and MUTCDC standards, ensuring full compliance on every project.

Training

Building Safer Teams From the Ground Up

Our hands-on training programs prepare workers and supervisors with the knowledge and confidence they need to keep every job site safe and compliant.

TCP Certification

Traffic Control Person certification courses that meet Nova Scotia's provincial standards, preparing workers for real-world flagging and traffic direction.

MUTCDC Standards

Courses covering the Manual of Uniform Traffic Control Devices for Canada, ensuring your team understands and applies national traffic control standards.

Refresher Courses

Keep your team's certifications current with refresher training that reinforces best practices, covers regulation updates, and ensures ongoing compliance.
